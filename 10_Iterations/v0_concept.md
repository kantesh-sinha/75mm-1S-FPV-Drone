# V0 — Concept Phase

The initial concept was generated with Blueprint.io and then reviewed as an engineering starting point.

Concept characteristics:
- 75 mm-class micro quad
- 1S battery
- F4 1S AIO-class FC
- integrated Serial ELRS
- Caddx Ant Nano-class analog camera
- separate analog VTX
- 0802 approximately 22K motors
- 40 mm propeller class

Blueprint outputs are retained as concept evidence, not as automatically verified engineering data.
