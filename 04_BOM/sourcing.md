# Sourcing

## Germany / EU sourcing strategy
Use EU/German FPV retailers where possible for the FC, motors, props, battery, camera, VTX and frame.

The goal is replaceability and realistic procurement rather than the lowest global price.

## Source record
For every purchased item record:
1. Manufacturer
2. Exact product name
3. Exact revision/version
4. Retailer
5. Country/region
6. Unit price
7. Date checked
8. Product URL
9. Electrical specification source

## Verification rule
A marketplace listing is not sufficient evidence for a critical voltage/current limit if a manufacturer document is available.
