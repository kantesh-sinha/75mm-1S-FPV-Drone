# Flight Testing

## Test progression
### F0 — Controlled hover
Short hover in a safe, open area.

### F1 — Basic translational flight
Low-speed pitch, roll and yaw movements.

### F2 — Indoor mission
Repeatable indoor route or maneuver set.

### F3 — Light outdoor mission
Only under suitable conditions and after bench verification.

## Record for every flight
- date/time
- battery ID
- battery start/end voltage
- duration
- flight mode
- environment
- pilot notes
- video/blackbox evidence
- abnormal behavior
- corrective action

Do not compare configurations without keeping battery, props and firmware conditions controlled.
