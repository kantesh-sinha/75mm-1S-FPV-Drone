# Propulsion Selection

## V1
- Motor: 0802, approximately 22,000 KV
- Quantity: 4
- Propeller: 40 mm, 3-blade
- Battery: 1S, approximately 450 mAh

## Engineering rationale
The 75 mm / 40 mm / 0802 combination is established in the tiny-whoop ecosystem. Community reports describe 0802 motors as a normal class for 75 mm / 40 mm builds, while also noting that higher blade count increases load and that 0802 performance can become limited outdoors.

BETAFPV's Meteor75 documentation provides a useful reference architecture: 75 mm, 0802 motors, 40 mm props, 1S 450 mAh BT2.0, and an F4 1S 5A Serial ELRS FC.

The exact motor model is not frozen until a sourceable part number is selected.
