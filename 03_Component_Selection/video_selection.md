# Video Subsystem Selection

## Camera
Caddx Ant Nano-class analog camera.

Requirements:
- compact form factor
- analog CVBS output
- compatible supply range for final wiring
- lightweight construction

## VTX
Separate analog VTX, AKK Nano3-class or equivalent.

The separate VTX is intentional. A modern 5-in-1 AIO could reduce mass and wiring, but separating camera, OSD path and VTX makes the signal chain visible for an electronics/system-integration portfolio.

## Design rule
The exact camera and VTX revision must be recorded in the BOM before assembly because marketplace listings can contain revision-specific electrical specifications.
