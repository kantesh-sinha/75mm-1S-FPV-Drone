# 11 — Mechanical Scope

Mechanical design is intentionally supporting documentation for V1.

## Scope
- commercial 75 mm-class frame
- component placement
- battery retention
- camera mounting
- VTX retention
- basic clearance and wiring routing

## Out of scope
- custom flight-controller PCB
- complex custom frame
- decorative enclosure
- extensive 3D-printed structures

Blueprint CAD is retained as concept/reference material. Exact dimensions will be verified against purchased hardware before assembly.
