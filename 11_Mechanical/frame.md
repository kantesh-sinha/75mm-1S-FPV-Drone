# Frame

## V1 mechanical requirement

Use a commercially available 75 mm-class whoop frame compatible with:
- four 0802-class motors
- 40 mm props
- 26 × 26 mm FC mounting
- camera/VTX packaging

The frame is supporting documentation, not the main engineering contribution.

## Verification

Before final assembly record:
- exact frame model
- wheelbase
- mass
- FC mounting pattern
- motor mounting compatibility
- prop clearance
- camera clearance
- battery location
