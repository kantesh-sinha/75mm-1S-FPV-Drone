# 01 — Requirements

## Mission
Develop a lightweight 75 mm-class 1S analog FPV quadcopter for indoor and light outdoor operation.

The project is primarily an embedded-electronics and system-integration demonstrator rather than a custom mechanical-design project.

## Engineering priorities
1. Germany/EU sourcing and replaceability
2. Electronics learning value
3. Assembly and debugging simplicity
4. Cost
5. Flight performance

## Constraints
- Exact FC revision must be identified before firmware flashing.
- Electrical connections must be verified against final hardware documentation.
- Flight performance is not claimed until measured.
- Mechanical CAD is supporting documentation rather than the primary engineering output.
