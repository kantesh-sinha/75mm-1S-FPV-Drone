# Assembly Procedure

## Stage A — Mechanical preparation
1. Confirm the 75 mm frame and 26 × 26 mm FC mounting compatibility.
2. Install motors without trapping wires.
3. Mount the FC in the intended orientation.
4. Position camera and VTX with antenna clearance.

## Stage B — Electrical integration
1. Inspect all boards and connectors.
2. Identify actual FC pad labels.
3. Wire motors to integrated ESC outputs.
4. Wire camera to documented CAM/power/GND interface.
5. Wire VTX to documented VTX/power/GND and SmartAudio where supported.
6. Inspect solder joints under magnification.
7. Check battery polarity and VBAT/GND continuity.

## Stage C — Firmware
Complete firmware setup before installing propellers.

## Stage D — Functional integration
Follow the bring-up sequence in bringup.md. Do not skip directly from soldering to flight.
