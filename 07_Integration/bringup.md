# 07 — Integration & Bring-up

Bring the system up in stages.

## 1. Visual inspection
Check orientation, solder quality, exposed conductors, connector polarity, motor routing and antenna clearance.

## 2. Continuity
Battery disconnected:
- VBAT ↔ GND unintended-short check
- solder-bridge inspection
- power wiring inspection

## 3. FC USB
Verify FC enumeration, Betaflight connection, IMU response and board orientation.

## 4. Receiver
Verify ELRS detection, binding, channel movement, arming behavior and failsafe.

## 5. Motors
**Propellers removed.** Test each motor independently for assignment, spin and direction.

## 6. Video
Verify camera power, analog image, OSD, VTX power/control and FPV receiver image.

## 7. Final assembly
Install antenna, battery retention and propellers only after bench checks pass.

## 8. First hover
Controlled environment, inspected battery and configured failsafe.
