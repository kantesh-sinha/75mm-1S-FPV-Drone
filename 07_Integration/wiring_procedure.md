# Wiring Procedure

## Order
1. Motor wiring
2. Camera wiring
3. VTX wiring
4. Antenna installation
5. Battery lead/connector inspection

## Wire-management rules
- Keep motor wires clear of propellers.
- Avoid exposed conductor near carbon/frame hardware.
- Provide strain relief where practical.
- Do not route the VTX antenna where it can contact a propeller.
- Keep signal and power wiring identifiable during debugging.

## Pre-power checklist
- [ ] Battery polarity confirmed
- [ ] No VBAT-to-GND short
- [ ] No solder bridge
- [ ] Correct VTX voltage confirmed
- [ ] Camera voltage confirmed
- [ ] Antenna installed before VTX RF operation
- [ ] Propellers removed
